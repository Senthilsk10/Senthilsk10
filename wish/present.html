Happy Birthday, Chitu

A small note for someone I completely misjudged.

Chithu,

Happy Birthday, angry bird.

I'll be honest — if you'd told me six months ago that I'd be writing you a birthday note this personal, I'd have laughed. Because for the longest time, in the story I was telling myself inside my head, you were the villain. The one standing between me and everything I wanted. I had built up this whole image of you — an angry bird, chappal picker, the controller — and I never once stopped to ask if any of it was actually true.

Then came Kovalam. Bala handing you the Nandhi statue, declaring you the Nandhi of my love life, and you rejecting it without even blinking. I was standing there trying not to laugh and also trying not to think about how accurate it was. The "C" keychain, the ring game, the random prizes we collected like idiots — I didn't expect that night to feel so easy. But it did.

And then the nail cutter scene at apartment. The moment you looked at me dead in the eye and said, "If you help them, I'll tell Abirami and she won't talk to you." I switched sides so fast it was embarrassing. Purely for survival. Absolutely no regrets.

But what I actually want to say — the real thing — is about 3 AM.

That walk downstairs when everyone else was asleep. The wind that made shuttle impossible. The two rounds back and forth at the apartment entrance, just talking. I came into that conversation still carrying three years of a story I had built in my head about you. And somewhere in that conversation, that whole story just fell apart. Quietly.

That conversation changed something in me for real.

And then Akkarai beach — when you called me over, when you told me that you both were going to the beach, and when I said I was tempted you simply said, "Okay, come." Just like that.

You gave me three hours I'll probably think about for years. The shells, the shore, the merry-go-round, the ring game, and the roll-the-ball game where she added her five to my row. I know you saw all of it. I know you were quietly making space for it to happen. I don't have the words to properly thank you for that.

So here's what I want to say on your birthday:

You're not the angry bird they made you out to be. You're loud, yes. You're intense, yes. You'll pick up a chappal if you have to — and honestly, that's probably fair.

But underneath all of that is someone who shows up for the people she loves with everything she has. Someone who stayed up until 4 AM talking to someone who didn't even fully deserve that conversation yet.

I'm glad I got to see that side of you properly. I'm glad you didn't let me stay wrong about you.

Happy Birthday, Chithu. You deserve a day as genuinely good as you actually are — not the version people assumed, but the real one.

And yes — I'm still picking "A." lettered key-chain

— Senthil
Made with appreciation, laughter, and a little less misunderstanding than before.
